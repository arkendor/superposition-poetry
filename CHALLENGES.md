# Superposition Poetry Challenges

Here are some challenges for you to explore the mechanics of Superposition Poetry!

Structure Challenges generally involve a structural constraint or quirk in how you make your poem,
while Literary Challenges go further by requiring this structural constraint to convey meaning and 
add to the central theme of the poem. 

You're welcome to share your attempts with other readers!

## Structure Challenges

1. Try making a poem with superpositions that don't depend on each other, but whose outcomes become correlated through a shared dependency.
2. Try making a poem with at least 4 superpositions.
3. Try making a poem whose superpositions are designed to be revealed back to front.
4. Try making a poem with at least one 0-probability superposition outcome.
5. Try making a poem that tells two substantially different stories using the same text.
   
## Literary Challenges
1. Try making a poem where the superposition probabilities don't actually represent probabilities, but serve as a vessel for a different form of storytelling.
2. Try making a poem where all superpositions must have equal probability between all possible outcomes in each conditional matrix row.
3. Try making a poem where the amount of superpositions, the placement of the superpositions, or the dependency graph carries meaning.
4. Try making a poem where at least one 0-probability superposition outcome carries meaning.
5. Try making a poem where the order in which superpositions are presented carries meaning; the reader revealing {2} then checking the {1} probabilities should produce a different narrative experience to the converse.
6. Try making a poem where the absence of a superposition or a dependency carries meaning, in a way other than it being interesting that a superposition could have also worked.
7. Try making a poem with exactly 1 superposition, with exactly 2 possible outcomes. What's the greatest amount of artistic meaning you can squeeze out of these limited resources?

And of course, feel free to come up with your own challenges and share those as well.
